## 🇬🇧 English

### v3.10.1
- ⏱️ Your last load shows next to **DROP IN**.

- 🔇 No ollie sound when the load ends.

### v3.9 – v3.9.19: direct load from ~16.6 s to ~7 s
- 🧩 **skate3-direct**: an in-game patch (`xinput9_1_0.dll`) that comes and goes with *Straight to your save*, for skate3recomp v2.0.2 only.
- 🚫 EA logo done at 0.03 s.
- 🏎️ No FPS cap while loading.
- ⏱️ Press Start without its minimum wait and with START instantly.
- 😴 Loading threads without 1 ms naps.
- 💓 Game tick at 4 ms during the short loading steps.
- 🖤 One-frame fades and the graphics card at high priority until you reach the game.
- 🌅 Preload also keeps the exe, the DLLs and the shader cache in memory.
- 📜 `skate3-direct.log` with how long each step took.
